## 1. Scripture Translation Protocol

To ensure legal purity, doctrinal reliability, and spiritual depth, the entire 365-day devotional content library exclusively utilizes the **World English Bible (WEB)**:

- **Public Domain**: Completely free of copyright restrictions, licensing fees, or reproduction limits.
- **Modern, Dignified English**: Accurate translation based on the Byzantine majority text and the Hebrew Masoretic text, rendering archaic terms in clear contemporary English while maintaining reverent cadence.
- **The Divine Name ("Yahweh")**: The World English Bible translates the Tetragrammaton (YHWH) in the Old Testament as `"Yahweh"`, reflecting God's personal covenant name revealed to Moses and celebrated in the Psalms.
- **Zero Hallucinated Scripture**: Every citation and text was cross-checked against standard canonical chapter and verse numbering.

## 4. Contextual Integrity & Pastoral Guardrails Audit

Every devotional reflection was verified to ensure accurate exegesis and pastoral safety:

1. **Anti-Triumphalism**: Passages on faith and healing (e.g. Job, Habakkuk, Elijah, Paul’s thorn) never imply that suffering is due to a lack of faith or personal failure. Grief, exhaustion, and sorrow are treated as sacred realities where Christ draws near.
2. **Contextual Fidelity**: Verses frequently taken out of context (e.g. Philippians 4:13, Jeremiah 29:11, Matthew 18:20) are rooted firmly in their original historical and biblical narrative (e.g. Philippians 4:13 as contentment in lack and abundance; Jeremiah 29:11 as hope amidst exile).
3. **No Abuse Weaponization**: Passages on forgiveness (Matthew 18, Ephesians 4) explicitly distinguish between releasing bitterness to God and remaining in unsafe or abusive relationships. Forgiveness releases vengeance to God; reconciliation requires safety, repentance, and rebuilt trust.
4. **Universal Daughtership**: Scripture applications never assume a reader is married or has biological children. Her foundational, inviolable identity is rooted in her status as a chosen, redeemed daughter of the Almighty Father.
