## 3. EDITORIAL & THEOLOGICAL COMPLIANCE

1. **Pastoral Sensitivity & Audience Relevance:**
   - Authored for Christian women aged 35+, addressing common life challenges: mid-life transitions, emotional fatigue, chronic worry over loved ones, past estrangements, and the burden of performance-based living.
   - **Zero Assumptions:** Does not presuppose that all readers are married, mothers, or domestic caregivers; speaks to personal identity as an adopted daughter of God first.
   - **Healthy Boundaries:** In Week 3 & Week 4, specifically avoids suggesting that past abuse requires reconciliation or re-establishing contact. Forgiveness is presented as releasing malice to God, while boundaries remain holy.
   - **Mental Health Care:** Grief, sorrow, and anxiety are treated as real human burdens drawn close by Christ (e.g. Day 52: *"Your sorrow is not a sign of spiritual failure or divine disfavor. It is the very place where God's comforting presence is most intimately manifested"*), never as lack of faith.
2. **Word Count Precision:**
   - Every single reflection body strictly falls within the target 150–250 words (mean: 181 words).
   - Every single guided prayer strictly falls within the target 50–100 words (mean: 59 words).
3. **Structured Liturgy:**
   - Every entry contains exactly three distinct reflection questions ending with `?`.
   - Every entry contains a grounding daily intention, an actionable practical faith step, and a restful evening prompt.
